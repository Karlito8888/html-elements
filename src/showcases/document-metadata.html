Sunrise Bakery

This page looks simple, but its head is busy. None of it is drawn on the page:

  • The <title> is the name in the browser tab above.
  • The <link> gives that tab its round yellow icon.
  • The <style> paints these warm colors.
  • The <meta> elements give information about the page: its character encoding, how it fits a phone screen, a short description.
  • The <base> makes short links point to MDN and open in a new tab: try this link.
  • All of them sit inside <head>, which holds what visitors don't see.

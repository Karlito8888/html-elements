Pancake recipe

Revised on November 2: green is new, red was removed.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 200 g of sugar 50 g of sugar
  • 250 ml of milk
  • A pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Whisk everything together.
  2. Wait one hour.
  3. Cook on a hot pan.

New tip: let the first pancake fail, it warms up the pan.

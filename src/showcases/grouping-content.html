House plant care

Five minutes a week keep a house plant happy. Here is everything in one place.

    •

    What you need

    • A pot with a hole at the bottom
    • Potting soil
    • A watering can

    Every week

    1. Touch the soil.
    2. If it is dry, water slowly.
    3. Turn the pot a quarter turn.

    Words to know

    Repotting
    Moving a plant into a bigger pot.
    Drainage
    Letting extra water flow out of the pot.
